`legacy_ema_pit_classification_h06_top05_v1`

Specification:

`configs/research/locked_legacy_ema_challenger_v1.json`

The frozen rule is:

- exact Legacy winning EMA pairs available point-in-time at the end of train;
- XGBoost classification of the future top 10% excess-return rank;
- 6-month label horizon;
- raw positive-class score for stock ordering;
- top 5 equal-weight monthly portfolio;
- next-month realized trading return;
- 10 bps multiplied by turnover;
- no tuning.

The 2013-2025 history was used to select this rule. It remains a useful
out-of-sample walk-forward, but it is not an untouched confirmatory holdout.

## Confirmatory diagnostics

### Paired temporal bootstrap

10,000 circular moving-block samples, with 12-month blocks:

| comparator | annualized mean-return difference | 95% CI | Sharpe difference | 95% CI |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| S&P 500 | +19.47 pp | +9.70 to +30.02 pp | +0.009 | -0.453 to +0.401 |
| Legacy | +16.30 pp | +3.88 to +28.90 pp | +0.096 | -0.326 to +0.499 |

The probability of a non-positive annualized mean difference is `0.00%`
against the S&P 500 and `0.42%` against Legacy. The corresponding probabilities
for the Sharpe difference are `50.35%` and `34.49%`.

Conclusion: the active return is persistent in this history, but superiority
in risk-adjusted performance is not statistically established.

### Deflated Sharpe

The raw annualized Sharpe is `0.969`. Using 162 autonomous combinations as the
multiple-testing count:

- expected maximum annualized Sharpe under the null: `0.781`;
- return skewness: `0.905`;
- Pearson kurtosis: `5.573`;
- Deflated Sharpe probability: `76.10%`.

This is supporting evidence, not a conventional 95% confirmation.

### Meta walk-forward selection

At each January, the selector considers 108 autonomous combinations:

- feature modes: broad, exact EMA only, exact EMA plus;
- objectives: classification, regression, ranking;
- horizons: 1, 3, 6, 12 months;
- top N: 5, 10, 20.

It selects the highest net Sharpe measured only over the preceding 36
out-of-sample months and holds that choice for the following calendar year.

Evaluation: January 2018 through October 2024, 82 months.

| strategy | total return | CAGR | Sharpe | max drawdown |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| temporal meta-selector | +167.8% | 15.5% | 0.589 | -33.0% |
| S&P 500 | +138.6% | 13.6% | 0.829 | -23.9% |
| Legacy | +133.3% | 13.2% | 0.684 | -23.4% |

The selection procedure slightly beats the comparators in raw return but not
in Sharpe or drawdown. It never selects the final 6-month/top-5 champion
exactly. This weakens any claim that the exploratory winner was an obvious,
stable choice through time.

## Decision

Status:

`paper_challenger_not_production_ready`

Evidence for continuation:

- positive active mean-return bootstrap intervals;
- robustness to costs;
- positive performance in 10 of 12 annual folds;
- signal family remains exact EMA, as intended.

Evidence against promotion:

- no untouched mature holdout;
- Deflated Sharpe probability only 76.1%;
- Sharpe-difference bootstrap intervals include zero;
- meta-selector underperforms Legacy and the S&P 500 on Sharpe;
- material sector concentration;
- high probability bins are sparse.

Next step:

1. generate and archive prospective monthly scores under the locked challenger
   id;
2. do not retune this version;
3. add volatility/downside heads as a separately versioned model;
4. evaluate risk sizing without changing the locked alpha ordering;
5. require a genuinely new mature holdout before production promotion.
